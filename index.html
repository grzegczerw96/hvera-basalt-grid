S.01About us

Hvera is a geothermal engineering company. We find heat underground, drill down to it and turn it into power and warm homes, from Reykjanes to the Rift Valley.

S.02Services

Six disciplines, one contract. From the first survey to steady output.

All services
01
Exploration

Magnetotelluric surveys, gas geochemistry and slim holes that map a reservoir before the first rig arrives.

02
Deep drilling

Directional wells to five kilometres with in-house rigs rated for 400 °C and high-pressure steam.

03
Well testing

Flow, injection and tracer tests that size every well and set its long-term output.

04
Plant EPC

Flash and binary plants from 5 to 120 MW, designed, built and commissioned by one team.

05
District heating

Exchangers, pipelines and pumping stations that carry spent brine heat into cities.

06
Operations

Remote monitoring, scaling control and workovers that keep plants above 95% availability.
